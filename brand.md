## Typography — Instrument Serif + Inter + JetBrains Mono

- **Display:** Instrument Serif, reserved for the main empty-state or hero statement.
- **Interface and body:** Inter.
- **Numbers, addresses, hashes, latency, and code:** JetBrains Mono with tabular numerals.

The fonts are self-hosted by `next/font/google` through `frontend/src/app/layout.tsx`. Tailwind utilities are exposed as `font-serif`, `font-sans`, and `font-mono`.

| Role | Tailwind classes | Use |

## Gradients

Two restrained gradients are available as `bg-brand-bg` and `bg-brand-accent`.

- `bg-brand-bg`: page-level or empty-state depth. Use at most once per screen.
- `bg-brand-accent`: small accent bars or decorative marks only.
- Do not place body text or transaction controls on the accent gradient.
- Do not animate either gradient.

## Tone and voice

### Use

Be precise, sparse, and number-forward. Prefer “2 risks found in 184 ms” over “Your powerful scan is ready.” Let playful character appear through the mascot, dry microcopy, and small moments—not through financial claims.

### Avoid

Avoid “revolutionary,” “game-changing,” “unlock,” “seamless,” and “easy.” Do not manufacture urgency, overpromise safety, or present a bot result as a substitute for an audit.

### Example

> Two risk signals found. Review the evidence before interacting.

- The approved nine-pose process sequence is `WhatsApp Image 2026-10-29 at 9.55.19 PM.jpeg`.
- Long-running work uses this order: idle, look around, prepare, jump, mid-air, spin, processing, almost there, back to idle.
- The loop lasts about 1.25 seconds and follows the supplied one-second reference clip's stop-motion rhythm.
- Reduced-motion users receive the stable idle pose and the same written status.
- Every loader pairs the character with a specific status message; the mascot never replaces progress text.

## Usage rules

**Do:**

- Use semantic tokens instead of hardcoded component colors.
- Keep a 4 px spacing grid and 40 px minimum interactive targets.
- Use visible focus rings and test keyboard navigation.
- Implement loading, empty, error, and success states.
- Verify every screen at 375 px, 768 px, and 1280 px.

**Don't:**

- Copy GrokBot’s mascot, logo, or exact proprietary assets.
- Mix multiple gradients on one screen.
- Use serif text for dense UI or data.
- Animate with `transition: all`.
- use an unapproved specialist bot implementation.
